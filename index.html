The best pre-sale renovation is the one buyers can see. Everything else is a cost.

This guide sorts every pre-sale job into four calls, shows you what buyers are paying for right now, and ends with a free walkthrough of your own home.

01The rule

Buyers pay for what they see. Inspectors find the rest.

Every pre-sale job sits on two scales: what it costs you in money and time, and what it does for a buyer at the open home. Where a job lands tells you what to do with it.

03Buyers now

What buyers are looking for in late 2026

The market has turned since May. Values have eased, borrowing is tight, and buyers have more homes to choose from. That changes what they will pay for.

Buyers are borrowing to their limit

Serviceability caps mean the deposit and the loan go on the purchase. A home that needs work after settlement asks for money many buyers no longer have.

Buyers are comparing more homes

With more listings to choose from, buyers shortlist online. The homes that look ready in the photos are the ones they book to see.

Every report item is a renegotiation

When prices are soft, a fault found after a price is agreed gives the buyer a reason to ask for less. Fixed before listing, it never comes up.

    06Queensland

    Three Queensland rules before you list

    Each one applies when you sell, and each takes time to organise. Start them in week six.

    1 Jan 2022

    Smoke alarms to the current standard

    A home sold in Queensland needs photoelectric, interconnected smoke alarms in every bedroom, in hallways that connect bedrooms, and on every level. From 1 January 2027 the standard applies to every home in the state.

    Book a licensed electrician to check and upgrade.

    Pool

    Pool safety certificate

    If the property has a pool or spa, you either provide a pool safety certificate, or give the buyer a Form 36 notice before the contract is signed. With a Form 36, the buyer has 90 days after settlement to get the certificate.

    Book a licensed pool safety inspector early. Repairs can follow an inspection.

    1 Aug 2025

    Seller disclosure statement

    Sellers must give buyers a signed disclosure statement (Form 2) with the prescribed certificates and searches before the buyer signs the contract.

    Instruct your solicitor or conveyancer before the campaign starts.

    General information only, current as at October 2026. It is not legal advice. Confirm your obligations with your solicitor, conveyancer or a licensed inspector.
